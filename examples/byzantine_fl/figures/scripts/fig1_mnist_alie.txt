This is Figure 1: detection accuracy round by round on MNIST under the Advanced Lie attack. The horizontal axis is the federated round, one through thirty. The vertical axis is detection accuracy in percent. Blue is classical MultiKrum, orange is the QUBO method solved by simulated annealing. Because there are fifteen clients and three attackers, only four values are possible at any round: 60 percent means no attacker caught, 73.33 means one, 86.67 means two, and 100 means all three. The blue line is a sawtooth with no trend, touching 100 at a few isolated rounds, sitting at 73.33 or 86.67 most of the time, and falling to the 60 percent floor twice, at rounds eight and twenty four. The orange line spends most of the run pinned at 100 percent, dropping to 86.67 for short stretches and once, at round three, to 73.33. Averaged over the thirty rounds this is 81.33 percent for classical against 95.11 for the QUBO method. The difference in steadiness matters as much as the difference in means: a defense that oscillates lets poison through on a regular schedule.
