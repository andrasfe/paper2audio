This is Figure 2: detection accuracy round by round on MNIST under the Sparse Lie attack, the same axes as before, blue for classical MultiKrum and orange for the QUBO method. This is the most revealing plot in the paper. The blue line starts well, at 86.67 percent for the first two rounds, meaning two of the three attackers caught, then falls to 73.33 and dips to the 60 percent floor at round eleven. It recovers briefly around round seventeen and then goes flat at 60 percent from round eighteen to the end of the run, thirteen consecutive rounds of catching no attackers at all. That is not underperformance, it is collapse, and it compounds: once poisoned updates enter the model, the honest gradients respond to a corrupted model and the attacker's job gets easier every round. The orange line does something completely different. It is flat at 73.33 percent for the first fifteen rounds, one attacker caught every round, then steps up to 86.67 at round sixteen and holds it for the rest of the run. It never reaches 100, so it never catches all three, but it never degrades. The averages are 66.67 percent classical against 80.89 for the QUBO method.
