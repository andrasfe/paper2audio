This is Figure 6: detection accuracy round by round on FashionMNIST under the Sparse Lie attack, blue for classical MultiKrum and orange for the QUBO method. Like Figure 5, its value lies in the comparison: set it beside Figure 2, the same attack on MNIST, and the shapes match. The blue line begins at 86.67 percent for the first couple of rounds, decays through 73.33, and then settles at the 60 percent floor, catching none of the three attackers for the long tail of the run. The orange line holds a flat 73.33 percent through the first two thirds of the run and then steps up to 86.67 percent near round twenty, holding that to the end. The averages are 65.78 percent for classical MultiKrum against 78.22 percent for the QUBO method, a gain of 12.4 points, close to the 14.2 point gain on MNIST. Two different image domains, one shared architecture, and the same two failure signatures: a classical defense that collapses to the floor as training proceeds, and a directional criterion that starts modest, improves once, and never degrades.
