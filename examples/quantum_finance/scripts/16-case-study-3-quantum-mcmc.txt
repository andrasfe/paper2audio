Now case study three, quantum enhanced Markov chain Monte Carlo, which delivers both the strongest quantum favorable measurement in the whole program and an honest null result on the financial side, plus the program's best story about scientific self correction. Same deal as before: full math, full implementation.

Background. Markov chain Monte Carlo, M C M C, is the workhorse for sampling from complicated probability distributions. Here the target is the Boltzmann distribution over portfolio configurations: at temperature T, each configuration gets probability proportional to the exponential of minus its energy divided by T. At low temperature this concentrates on near optimal configurations, so sampling from it is a soft version of optimizing, and it is also exactly what statistical physics and Bayesian computation need daily. The classic algorithm is Metropolis Hastings, a loop with two halves. First, from the current configuration x, propose a new configuration y, for example by flipping one randomly chosen switch. Second, accept or reject: downhill moves always, uphill moves with probability equal to the exponential of minus the energy increase over temperature. Run long enough and the chain visits configurations with exactly the Boltzmann probabilities. The catch is that phrase, long enough. On a rugged landscape at low temperature, a single flip chain gets trapped in a valley for astronomically many steps, because escaping requires a run of individually rejected uphill moves.

Trapping has an exact mathematical measure, and this study's whole design is built on computing it without approximation. A Markov chain is a transition matrix P, where the entry in row x, column y is the probability of stepping from configuration x to configuration y. The chain's long run behavior is governed by this matrix's eigenvalues: the largest is always exactly one, and the distance between one and the second largest magnitude, called the spectral gap, delta, sets the relaxation time, which scales as one over delta. A gap of ten to the minus twelve means the chain needs on the order of ten to the twelve steps to forget its starting point: frozen, in practice. The implementation exploits a classical fact: if the chain satisfies detailed balance, the matrix D to the half, P, D to the minus half is symmetric, where D is the diagonal matrix of stationary probabilities, so its full spectrum can be computed by dense symmetric eigendecomposition, exactly, no sampling, no iteration. For systems up to twelve spins, the full two to the twelve by two to the twelve matrix, sixteen million entries, is tractable. One war story from the code: the standard iterative sparse eigensolver was tried first and stalled, one instance sat for fifty seven minutes, because at cold temperature the top of the spectrum becomes a near degenerate cluster, the worst case for iterative methods; the fix was to switch to full dense diagonalization, slower per matrix in theory, bombproof in practice.

The quantum idea, from a twenty twenty three Nature paper by Layden and coauthors, is minimal and elegant: keep the entire classical Metropolis loop, replace only the proposal. To propose from configuration x, load x into qubits, evolve for a short time t under a Hamiltonian H that blends the problem with a mixer, then measure, obtaining a proposal y. Here is the exact blend used, matching the reference: H equals, one minus gamma, times alpha, times the problem Hamiltonian whose diagonal is the energy function, plus gamma times the transverse mixing term, which is the sum over spins of the operator that flips that spin. The constant alpha is a normalizer, the square root of, n divided by the sum of squared couplings plus squared fields, which puts the problem and mixer terms on comparable energy scales regardless of instance; gamma is drawn uniformly between zero point two five and zero point six, and the evolution time t uniformly between two and twenty, with the proposal matrix averaged over six random draws of the pair, exactly the randomization the reference prescribes. In the simulation, the unitary is computed as an exact matrix exponential, and the proposal probability from x to y is the squared magnitude of the y x entry. Two properties make this practical. First, symmetry: because the Hamiltonian is a real symmetric matrix, the probability of proposing y from x equals that of x from y, and a symmetric proposal drops out of the Metropolis acceptance ratio entirely, so the chain converges to exactly the right Boltzmann distribution no matter how strange the proposal is. Second, noise robustness follows from the same fact: hardware imperfections deform which y gets proposed, but never the correctness of the accept reject step, so noise costs speed, never truth. Why should quantum proposals help at all? Under Hamiltonian evolution, amplitude flows preferentially between configurations of similar total energy; from a deep valley, that means jumps to other deep valleys, arbitrarily many spin flips away, precisely the moves a single flip chain cannot compose at low temperature.

The experimental design. Two ensembles. The control: Sherrington Kirkpatrick spin glasses, the canonical rugged landscape, with couplings drawn from a Gaussian and scaled by two over the square root of n, and random fields; this is the ensemble where the Nature effect must appear, or the pipeline is broken. The finance ensemble: sub instances of the one hundred portfolio calm window QUBOs from case study one, the certified hard family, converted to spin variables by the standard substitution, x equals one minus s over two, which maps the QUBO matrix into pairwise couplings J, local fields h, and a constant. Four proposals compete under the identical Metropolis rule: the quantum proposal; single flip, which picks one of n spins uniformly; uniform, which proposes any configuration; and random k flip, which flips a binomially distributed number of spins spread evenly across Hamming distances. Instance sizes six through twelve, five seeds each and eight at the largest size, fifty seven instances in all. Every cell computes the exact spectral gap at three temperatures, and two validity checks are enforced everywhere: rows of the transition matrix sum to one within ten to the minus nine, and detailed balance, the stationarity equation pi P equals pi, holds to within nine times ten to the minus sixteen, machine precision.

And now the confession, elevated to a feature. The first version of this study failed its own positive control gate. The gate demanded that on the spin glass control at the lowest temperature, the quantum proposal show a clear relaxation advantage; instead its fitted slope came out worse than single flip, zero point four one against zero point zero nine per spin. The frozen rule engaged: no finance verdict may be issued from a pipeline that fails its control. The diagnosis is a lovely lesson in dimensional analysis. Version one set temperature per instance as a multiple of the median single flip energy change; but on dense instances that quantity grows like the square root of n, because flipping one spin against n minus one Gaussian couplings produces an energy change of that scale. So larger instances were simulated effectively hotter, which flattens the classical slope and steepens the quantum one, corrupting exactly the size comparison the study exists to make. Meanwhile the absolute gaps at fixed size were reproducing the Nature effect all along, at twelve spins and cold temperature, quantum gap four point three times ten to the minus two versus single flip two point nine times ten to the minus three, fifteen times better. The pre registered response: document the failure, register version two with exactly two amendments, an intensive temperature scale, T equals J r m s times two to the minus k for rung k, where J r m s is the root mean square coupling, a size independent yardstick, plus one extra instance size for fit stability, and re run everything from scratch. Nothing else changed: same ensembles, same proposals, same gates, same decision rule.

The results, at the coldest temperature, a quarter of J r m s. Version two's control gate passed decisively, and the figure tells it in one glance: cost is the logarithm base two of one over the spectral gap, so slopes are exponents per added spin. On the spin glasses, single flip explodes as temperature falls, its exponent climbing zero point six seven, then one point two one, then two point two four per spin down the three temperature rungs, each added spin at the coldest rung multiplying relaxation time by nearly five. The quantum proposal's exponent stays essentially flat across the same ladder: zero point two seven, zero point three two, zero point three one. That endpoint comparison, zero point three one versus two point two four, is the roughly seven fold exponent advantage, the cleanest reproduction of the Layden effect in the program and the strongest quantum favorable measurement anywhere in this paper. The generic proposals sit in between around slope one, which is itself informative: the quantum proposal is not winning by mere nonlocality, since uniform and random k flip are fully nonlocal and still lose to it decisively at cold temperature.

Now the finance panel, same axes, and the picture collapses, but read it with the frozen decision rule in hand. The rule said: quantum wins a temperature rung if its slope is at most three quarters of the best classical slope and the gap between them exceeds their combined standard errors, with two of three rungs needed for a positive verdict. Measured, rung by rung: quantum zero point one six six plus or minus zero point one zero five, against best classical zero point two one five plus or minus zero point zero four four; then zero point one six four against zero point two zero zero; then zero point two four nine against zero point zero two eight, where at that coldest rung the best classical is single flip at essentially slope zero. No rung satisfies the win condition; the frozen verdict is no evidence. And the diagnosis is visible in the raw numbers: single flip relaxes with slopes of zero point zero three to zero point two two at every temperature on these instances. Nothing traps. Finance derived sub instances at twelve spins or fewer are simply not glassy, so there is no classical bottleneck for the quantum proposal to relieve.

This is the scale mismatch conclusion, and it ties the whole program together. Case study one established, with certification failures and annealing gaps, that the hardness of the one hundred portfolio calm family is a collective property that turns on around thirty two coupled assets and is not inherited by small sub instances, whose problems certify in seconds. Exact spectral gap computation, needing the full two to the n matrix, is confined below about twelve to fourteen spins. The sizes where the method is exactly measurable and the sizes where the financial hardness lives do not intersect. Yet. The paper names the bridging experiment precisely: give up exact spectra, estimate mixing times by sampling, autocorrelation and coupling from the past bounds on actual running chains, with the quantum proposal simulated by sparse matrix exponential action, which is feasible at sixteen to thirty two spins, exactly the range where finance hardness switches on. That experiment would be a new pre registration, and nothing in version two was tuned toward it.

One honesty clause to carry out of this chapter, pre registered and applied even to the successful control result: all these exponents count chain steps. One quantum step means running a circuit whose depth grows with n times t, microseconds to milliseconds on any foreseeable hardware; one classical single flip step is order n floating point operations, nanoseconds. So even the beautiful seven fold exponent advantage is evidence about the asymptotic mixing structure of cold rugged landscapes, that is, about future advantage once exponents dominate constants at scale, not a present day wall clock claim at any size measured here. The paper never lets the reader forget the difference.
