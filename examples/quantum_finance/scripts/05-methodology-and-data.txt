One more piece of methodology and then we get to the science. Every study in this paper was pre-registered. That means the authors wrote down, in committed documents, before running anything: which data windows they would use, which metrics they would compute, which thresholds would count as success or failure, and which controls they would run. Why does this matter? Because research has a disease called forking paths: if you decide after seeing the data which analyses to report, you can nearly always find something that looks like a discovery. Pre-registration removes that freedom. The paper takes it seriously enough that, as you will hear in case study three, when one of their own quality gates failed, they reported the failure, documented a single amendment, and re-ran the whole study rather than quietly patching it. They also ran adversarial controls, meaning deliberately scrambled versions of the data designed to kill the effect if it is real, or expose it if it is an artifact. And they maintained honesty clauses separating what was actually measured from what was extrapolated. No quantum hardware time was used anywhere; every quantum object was either simulated exactly on classical machines or resource-counted from verified circuit constructions.

The data deserves a sentence. Everything runs on the Kenneth French data library, a famous public dataset of United States stock market portfolios with daily returns from nineteen twenty six to twenty twenty six, a full century. Two universes are used: forty nine industry portfolios, one per industry like steel, banking, software, and one hundred portfolios formed by sorting stocks into a ten by ten grid on company size and valuation. Time is sliced into windows of one hundred twenty six trading days, about half a year. Four headline windows anchor everything: calm two thousand six and calm twenty nineteen, versus the global financial crisis window starting autumn two thousand eight, and the covid crash window of spring twenty twenty. On top of those, over a hundred rolling windows trace how everything evolves through time.
