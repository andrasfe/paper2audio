Before the objections chapter, a short interlude that I think of as reading the numbers like a referee. A pre registered program lives and dies by what happens when measured values land near, on, or across its frozen thresholds, and this program had seven such moments. Walking through them one by one is the fastest way to internalize both the findings and the discipline, because each moment forced a choice between the honest reading and the flattering one, in public, with the rule already on file.

Moment one: the distance correlation verdict that reads inconclusive while being the strongest result in the paper. The frozen rule for case study one anticipated two outcomes: hardness rises in crises beyond the noise scale, call ruggedization; or differences stay within noise, call stability. The measurement delivered a third thing: crisis concentration tighter than calm by more than two and a half times the bootstrap noise scale of zero point two four six, but on the opposite side from the hypothesis, with solving times easier by factors of twelve to a thousand. The rule, having no branch for a significant reverse effect, formally returns inconclusive. The referee's lesson: the paper reports the formal verdict and the substantive finding side by side, rather than quietly rewriting the rule to claim a cleaner win. A frozen rule that cannot express your discovery is a documentation problem, never a license to improvise.

Moment two, the quiet twin: the mutual information arm. Calm and crisis concentration differed by zero point one zero against a noise scale of zero point one five six, comfortably inside noise, and solving times were uniformly trivial. Verdict, stability confirmed, exactly as the rule intended. Worth noticing because null branches that fire correctly are the evidence that the machinery is calibrated, not just dramatic.

Moment three: the twenty five percent gate miss on resource counting. Two independent T gate counts, analytic and constructed, were required to agree within twenty percent; they disagreed by twenty five to twenty six. The gate failed, and the failure was handled by the book: diagnose the cause, the circuit library's generic controlled ripple decomposition is fatter than the literature construction, then adopt the resolution that weakens your own conclusion, use the smaller count, which makes the quantum machine look cheaper and the negative verdict harder to reach. A miss processed in the direction adverse to your thesis is worth more credibility than a pass.

Moment four: the scaling threshold graze in the universe extension. The pre registered bar for reviving the asymptotic quantum case was a fitted annealing exponent above zero point two five per variable. The covid window came in at zero point two four six. Four thousandths under the bar. The frozen answer is no revival, and the paper says exactly that, then adds the honest physics: the fit points are convex, medians at forty variables jump several doublings above the trend, so the straight line slope likely understates the true rate, and the right response is a deeper follow up, which became the door two study, rather than a re fit. Near threshold discipline in one sentence: the rule decides the verdict, the residuals decide the follow up.

Moment five: door two itself, the split decision. The follow up rule asked whether the deep size slope exceeded one and a half times the shallow slope, window by window. Calm two thousand six: zero point two one three rising to zero point three nine three, ratio one point eight, confirmed, hardness genuinely accelerating, annealing cost doubling every two and a half variables. Calm twenty nineteen: zero point one seven eight to zero point two three one, ratio one point three, below the bar, flag retired. Same pipeline, same rule, opposite outcomes across windows, and both reported. Heterogeneous verdicts are what real data looks like; uniform sweeps are what curve fitting looks like.

Moment six: the nested simulation cell that failed its own bar while producing astronomical numbers. Cell three's premium relative tolerance pushed the inner path count to four and a half billion, seemingly a triumph for the criterion, until you check it against the cell's own pre registered inner hardness bar of seven point one billion, which it misses by a factor of one point six. The formal branch says: still short. And the substantive analysis says more: with both conventions producing opposite absurdities on the same book, the verdict was declared convention dominated, and the series was stopped rather than tuning a third convention that would have known the answer it wanted. Declining to run the experiment that can only flatter you is the rarest move in this business.

Moment seven: the no evidence versus inconclusive distinction in case study three. The frozen taxonomy separates two ways to not win: inconclusive, the data cannot rank the proposals, and no evidence, the data affirmatively shows the quantum slope failing to beat the best classical slope, at the coldest temperature, with the quantum proposal not even leading the pack. The finance measurement landed in the second, stronger category, quantum at zero point two four nine against single flip at essentially zero, and the paper says no evidence, while simultaneously scoring the control ensemble as the program's strongest positive. Precision about which kind of null you obtained is what lets a program carry a positive and a negative in the same hand without either contaminating the other.

Now step back from the seven moments and notice the pattern in the whole scoreboard. Every close call, the twenty five percent miss, the zero point two four six graze, the one point six factor shortfall, was resolved by the pre committed rule, in the conservative direction, with the tension documented instead of dissolved. And every decisive number, the fifty to one hundred twenty times transfer failure, the five orders of magnitude cost gap, the seven fold control advantage, sits so far from its threshold that no reasonable rule variation changes it. That is the signature of findings that will replicate: the verdicts do not live near the thresholds, and the items that do live near thresholds are labeled as exactly what they are, follow up work. Keep that scoreboard in mind through the objections chapter next, because half of the objections dissolve against precisely this structure.
