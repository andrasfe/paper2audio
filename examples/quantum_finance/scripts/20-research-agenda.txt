So what should actually be done next? A good feasibility program does not end with verdicts; it ends with a costed queue of experiments, each designed so that either outcome is informative. This chapter is that queue: five concrete follow ups, in priority order, with their designs sketched and their decision stakes named, plus the standing checklist for anyone evaluating a quantum finance proposal from outside.

First in the queue, and the clear priority: the bridging experiment for quantum enhanced M C M C. Recall the gap it must close: the quantum proposal is validated on rugged landscapes up to twelve spins, financial hardness turns on above roughly thirty two, and exact spectral gaps cannot follow, because the transition matrix doubles in size with every added spin. The bridge is to give up exact spectra and measure mixing the way practitioners do: run the actual chains, many of them, and estimate relaxation from integrated autocorrelation times of energy and overlap observables, cross checked with coupling from the past style bounds that detect when chains started from opposite extremes have provably coalesced. The quantum proposal stays classically simulable in this range because you never need the full unitary matrix, only its action on the current configuration's state vector, which sparse matrix exponential methods deliver at sixteen, twenty four, even thirty two spins. The pre registration writes itself: the same four proposals, the intensive temperature ladder, sampled instances from the certified hard calm window family at sizes sixteen to thirty two, a positive control on spin glasses at the same sizes, and the same three quarters slope win rule. The stakes: this is the one experiment in sight that could produce the program's first genuinely positive finance verdict, evidence that the only validated quantum mixing advantage reaches the sizes where certified financial hardness actually lives. Either answer would be worth a paper.

Second: the premium homogeneous nested book for amplitude estimation. The nested cells died of a convention, not of physics: on a book whose option premiums span four orders of magnitude, the inner tolerance rule dominates the verdict, absolute tolerance trivializes the cheap options, relative tolerance explodes on them. The clean experiment holds premiums homogeneous, an at the money, path dependent book, Asian and lookback structures on comparable notionals, or better, obtains an actual desk's tolerance schedule, the numbers a real risk control function signs off on, and re evaluates the refined two level criterion: outer multiplier above ten to the five, and inner path counts far beyond the coherent overhead floor of roughly sixteen over the inner amplitude tolerance. Long dated Bermudan books with least squares Monte Carlo inner loops, and stochastic volatility capital models, are the named candidates most likely to clear both bars. The stakes: this either produces the first workload that legitimately passes the criterion, redirecting all quantum estimation effort toward it, or it establishes that even nesting cannot rescue estimation workloads, which would be the strongest negative in the series.

Third: stress the structural screen beyond long only equities. The physics chapter predicts where glassiness could hide: dependence structures without a single dominant factor. Concretely, run the identical pipeline, dependence matrices, calibrated QUBOs, certified or gap bounded baselines, coupling coefficient of variation, self averaging probe, on universes engineered to weaken the market factor: market neutral long short spread books, cross asset baskets mixing rates, credit, commodities and currencies, single name credit spreads where sector contagion competes with idiosyncratic default risk, and volatility surface deformations. The pre registered question is the same H one, and the honest expectation, from the factor mechanism, is that heterogeneity rises as factor dominance falls, with the interesting open question being whether any real universe crosses from awkward into genuinely glassy. Even a null delivers value: it would generalize the self averaging verdict from one asset class to the market as a whole.

Fourth: the deep n scaling ladder. The one hundred portfolio study saw annealing cost curves bending upward, doubling times shrinking to two and a half assets on calm two thousand six, before certification gave out near sixty variables. The follow up pushes reference quality optima into the eighty to one hundred twenty variable range by escalating the certification machinery: longer solver budgets, semidefinite relaxation bounds to certify gaps when exact proofs time out, and parallel restart ensembles large enough that the best found value stabilizes. The question, frozen in advance: does the accelerating exponent persist, saturate, or roll over? The stakes are the classical half of the whole story: if financial hardness genuinely accelerates with universe size, the classical baseline itself weakens at scale, and the crossover arithmetic for every quantum method changes; if it saturates, the classical fortress holds at every size that matters.

Fifth, cheapest, and continuous rather than one shot: hardware trigger maintenance. The criterion equation and the three screens turn quantum news into a five minute annual update instead of a strategy panic. The tracked quantities are few: logical error rates and code distances actually achieved, magic state production rates, loader constructions' measured T counts, and, for the sampling track, hardware demonstrations of the quantum proposal at growing depth. Each feeds a named constant, the per call cost in the crossover formula, the wall clock side of the mixing advantage, and the pre computed answer to, does this announcement change any verdict, replaces the current industry practice of re litigating strategy from headlines.

And for the listener who evaluates rather than runs experiments, the standing checklist, the paper's most portable export, in one breath each. Screen one, structural hardness: demand evidence that the target problem ensemble is non self averaging at deployment scale, certified baselines, controls attributing the hardness to structure rather than estimator noise; if instances are typical and benign, no optimizer, quantum or otherwise, is worth a premium. Screen two, cost ratio: for estimation claims, demand the measured classical per sample cost and the counted quantum per call cost in the same units on the same model, then read the crossover precision off the criterion equation; if it sits beyond any business requirement, stop. Screen three, scale overlap: demand that the sizes where the quantum method has been validated intersect the sizes where the problem is actually hard; a method proven at twelve variables and a problem hard at fifty is a research program, not a product. Three screens, all classical, all cheap, all pre registerable. Run them before the hardware pilot, and you will either save the pilot's budget or, far more valuably, know precisely why it deserves to be run.

That is the agenda. Two chapters remain: the whole program compressed to its numbers, and the closing argument.
