Quantum hardware is not commercially usable today; the value of a
use-case study is therefore not deployment but selection:
identifying, with measured constants, which workloads could ever repay
quantum investment and which never will. The three cases below were
chosen because each is the canonical pairing for its algorithm, each is
economically real, and each is testable end-to-end on public data.

-- Compact portfolio selection (QAOA) --

Select $k$ of $n$ assets that are jointly relevant to the market factor
and minimally redundant---index tracking, hedge-basket construction,
and feature selection all share this shape. The natural encoding is a
dependence-structure QUBO built from distance-correlation (DC) or
mutual-information (MI) matrices  [ref: samanta2025]. It is a good
quantum testbed because the instance ensemble is generated by the
market itself and varies with regime: a widely-voiced hypothesis holds
that crisis-period correlation structure makes these QUBOs rugged and
classically hard, which would be exactly the opening a quantum
optimizer needs. Whether that is true had not been measured.

-- Tail-risk measurement (QAE) --

Value-at-Risk and expected shortfall ($\cvar$) are computed by
Monte-Carlo simulation across the industry, at enormous aggregate
compute cost. QAE's quadratic speedup applies directly. It is a good
testbed because everything is quantifiable: the precision a risk desk
actually needs (basis points), the classical cost (measurable to the
nanosecond), and the quantum cost (countable to the $T$-gate)---so the
feasibility question reduces to arithmetic once the constants are
pinned down on a calibrated, backtested model.

-- Sampling on rugged landscapes (quantum MCMC) --

Wherever finance needs low-temperature samples or near-optimal
configurations on rugged objective surfaces---combinatorial allocation
under complex constraints, Bayesian posteriors with multimodal
structure---classical local-move MCMC freezes. Quantum-enhanced MCMC is
a good testbed because it is the one candidate whose advantage has
hardware evidence on the right landscape class, because correctness is
noise-robust, and because case study 1 supplies naturally-occurring
financial instances whose hardness is independently certified.
