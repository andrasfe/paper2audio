Data: Kenneth French daily portfolios---49 industries and 100
size/book-to-market portfolios (10$\times$10), 1926--2026, committed
with the code. Windows of 126 trading days define instrument ensembles:
calm (2006, 2019) versus crisis (GFC autumn 2008, COVID spring 2020),
plus rolling windows for trajectories. All QUBOs use one construction
(relevance/redundancy form, pool by relevance, soft cardinality with
calibrated penalty, unit normalization). Classical baselines: Gurobi
with exact eigenvalue-shift convexification at zero MIP gap
(certification), vectorized simulated annealing (SA) with
time-to-solution $\tts$ (sweeps $\times$ restarts to 99% success),
and replicated vectorized Monte Carlo. Every study's windows, metrics,
controls, and decision rules were frozen in committed pre-registrations
before results existed; the few amendments (all pre-result, all
cost- or artifact-driven) are documented in place, including one
positive-control gate failure that invalidated and corrected a study
design (Sec. [sec:cs3]).
