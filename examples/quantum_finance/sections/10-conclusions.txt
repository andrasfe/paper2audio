For the typical financial optimization use case, our evidence indicates
QAOA is the wrong tool for a structural reason, not an engineering one:
data-driven financial QUBOs are self-averaging---instance-to-
instance fluctuations shrink with size, typical instances are alike and
benign, and the heterogeneous, glassy structure that quantum
optimizers would need does not survive aggregation, least of all in
crises, which homogenize the dependence structure and make the
problems easier. Where residual hardness does appear (calm
periods, wide universes), classical annealing retains a decisive
scaling edge and the fixed-angle protocol fails its transfer gates.

This does not mean quantum computing will not be practical---in finance
or elsewhere. Two of our three case studies produced genuinely
quantum-favorable measurements: the QAE quadratic speedup observed
end-to-end on a data-calibrated oracle, and a $7\times$ mixing-exponent
advantage for quantum-enhanced MCMC on cold rugged landscapes,
reproduced under gates from independent hardware evidence. What our
program shows is that practicality is a workload-selection
problem with measurable acceptance criteria: per-scenario classical
cost exceeding $\sim$$10^{5}\times$ vectorized arithmetic for
estimation workloads; genuinely non-self-averaging, pool-scale rugged
structure for optimization and sampling workloads; and overlap between
the sizes where quantum methods can be validated and the sizes where
the target problem is hard. Financial workloads that clear these
screens were not found among the standard candidates tested here---but
the screens themselves, and the validated measurement machinery behind
them, are the constructive product: they tell practitioners exactly
what to measure before spending a dollar of quantum hardware time, and
they will identify the first genuine application if and when one
exists.

pre-registrations, checkpointed results ($\sim$700 cells/instances
across five studies), and amendment history are in the accompanying
repository; no QPU seconds were consumed.
