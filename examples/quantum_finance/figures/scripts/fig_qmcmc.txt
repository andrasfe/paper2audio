This figure shows the quantum enhanced M C M C result, two panels sharing one vertical axis. In both, the horizontal axis is instance size, six to twelve spins, and the vertical axis is relaxation cost, the logarithm base two of one over the spectral gap at the coldest temperature, so higher means exponentially slower mixing and the slope of each line is the exponent per added spin. Each small dot is one instance, shaded bands show the middle fifty percent, and lines connect the medians: purple circles for the quantum proposal, green squares for single flip, orange diamonds for random k flip, gray triangles for uniform. The left panel is the control ensemble of random spin glasses, and it shows the discovery: the green single flip line rockets upward with slope about two point two, while the purple quantum line stays nearly flat at slope about zero point three, the seven fold exponent advantage. The right panel is the finance derived instances, and every line is low and flat, single flip included. Nothing traps, so there is no bottleneck for the quantum proposal to relieve at these sizes, which is exactly the paper's scale mismatch conclusion.
