This chart compares the four headline market windows side by side, in three panels of bars. In every panel the horizontal axis lists the windows: calm two thousand six, the two thousand eight crisis, calm twenty nineteen, and the covid twenty twenty window, with blue bars for the distance correlation problems and red bars for the mutual information problems. The left panel shows the angle spread of trained Q A O A angles: the calm bars stand tall, around one to one and a half, while the crisis bars drop to roughly half a radian, meaning angles concentrate in crises. The middle panel shows classical solving difficulty, time to solution on a logarithmic scale, so each gridline is a factor of ten: the calm bars reach millions of annealing sweeps while the covid bar collapses to a few thousand, the twelve to one thousand fold easing. The right panel is the shuffle control: after permuting coupling values, the calm and crisis bars become similar, confirming that the regime effect lives in the coupling values themselves, not their arrangement.
